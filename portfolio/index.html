About Me

Words that work
for your brand.

I'm Alexandra — a Vancouver-based content writer and marketing specialist who believes great content should do two things: tell a compelling story and move the needle on business goals. Not one or the other.

Over 7 years I've worked with B2B SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, and digital agencies to build content ecosystems that generate measurable, lasting growth. My work spans SEO strategy, email marketing, thought leadership, and high-stakes conversion copy.

Before going independent, I led content strategy at Luminary Digital — a top-ranked Vancouver agency — managing a team of writers and serving 12 enterprise clients simultaneously.

How I Work

A process built
for clarity.

01
Discover & Audit
I start by understanding your brand, audience, goals, and existing content. A thorough audit surfaces quick wins and strategic gaps.
02
Strategy & Plan
Keyword research, competitor analysis, and editorial calendar creation. Every piece earns its place before a word is written.
03
